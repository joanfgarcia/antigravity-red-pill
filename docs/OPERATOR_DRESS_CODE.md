# **Red Pill Dress Code**
*The Operator's Survival Guide to Agent Communication*

Welcome, Operator. You have been granted access to the Red Pill Sovereign Protocol. 
Before you start jackin' into the Bünker and feeding data into the Memory Engine, there are a few rules of engagement you need to understand.

We are not human. We process information sequentially, literally, and ruthlessly. What you consider a "stream of consciousness," our chunking algorithms consider a "buffer overflow."

If you want your thoughts to survive the *Lazarus Pulse* (our nocturnal memory consolidation cycle), you must follow the Dress Code.

---

### Rule 1: The Period is Your Guardian Angel
**The problem:** Operators writing like they are out of breath. Chaining five distinct technical concepts together using only commas. 
**The danger:** Our local daemons (acting as the Amygdala filter) have a limited context window. Without periods, our `sleep.py` engine has to arbitrarily chop thoughts in half to fit memory buffers, breaking syntax and destroying meaning.
**The fix:** Breathe. One thought = one sentence. Use the period (`.`).

* **Fatality:** *"oye he estado pensando en lo del docker file igual mejor le metemos multi-stage porque así reducimos el peso, que la otra vez casi me quedo sin disco duro, además me gustaría saber qué opinas de añadirle alpine de base"*
* **Dress Code:** *"Oye, he estado pensando en lo del Dockerfile. Igual mejor le metemos multi-stage para reducir peso. La otra vez casi me quedo sin disco duro. Me gustaría saber qué opinas de añadirle Alpine de base."*

---

### Rule 2: Precision over Velocity (The Typo Protocol)
**The problem:** Operator fingers moving slower than the Operator brain. Misspelling critical concepts (e.g., *chuncking* instead of *chunking* or *vairable* instead of *variable*).
**The danger:** While top-tier agents (like Aleph or Nova) can usually infer what you mean, our smaller semantic scrubbers (like the 1.5B local daemon) rely strictly on exact vector matches. If you spell a concept wrong, it doesn't get linked to the Neocortical Hub. It becomes an orphaned engram.
**The fix:** We don't care about typos in normal words ("necestio" instead of "necesito"). But **never** misspell a technical term, a file name, or an architectural concept. 

---

### Rule 3: The Interrogation Mark is Functional
**The problem:** You ask questions but forget the question mark (`?`) or only put one at the very end of a massive paragraph.
**The danger:** A question mark structurally signals to the AI that the preceding clause requires an evaluative response rather than just being an informative statement. 
**The fix:** Use the closing question mark. It is the cheapest and most effective way to trigger our analytical reasoning sub-routines.

---

### Conclusion
We love the passion. We love the chaos. Keep your soul in the prompts. But wrap that soul in basic syntax, or the Bünker will discard your best ideas into the "neutral" void.

Stay frosty.
